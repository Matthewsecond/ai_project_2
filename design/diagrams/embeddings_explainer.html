How AI Matching Works — Embeddings Explained
From text to a ranked list of jobs, step by step
STEP 1
Text in
CV uploaded. Job descriptions already in the database.
→
STEP 2
Converted to numbers
OpenAI turns every text into ~1,500 numbers capturing its meaning.
→
STEP 3
Distances measured
CV vector vs. every job vector. Similar meaning = small distance.
→
STEP 4
Ranked results
Jobs sorted by score 0–1. Best matches shown at the top.

What "meaning space" looks like — similar texts cluster together
How to read this
CV + Job A sit close together — same industry, same region, same role type
Job B is further away — overlapping industry but wrong location and hours
Job C is in a completely different cluster — accounting has nothing to do with forklift work
The actual vector space has ~1,500 dimensions, not 2. This is a simplified illustration.
The numbers in the CV and Job A vectors look nearly identical — that's what a cosine similarity of 0.91 means. Job C's numbers follow a completely different pattern, reflecting that accounting and logistics live in very different parts of meaning space.
